01 / What we do

Better decisions for organisations doing meaningful work.

180 Degrees Consulting connects socially conscious organisations with driven student consultants.

At IIM Indore, we combine structured problem-solving, rigorous research, analysis, and close collaboration to make high-quality consulting accessible to organisations working for positive social and environmental change.

Every engagement is designed around a real decision. The organisation receives clear, evidence-based recommendations; our consultants build the judgement and skills to become thoughtful leaders.
